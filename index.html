Why OffChat

Made for the places networks give up

Networks fail exactly when lots of people are together. Every messenger assumes a connection and an account. OffChat assumes neither.

An arrow, never a map

Find Friend shows the direction and distance to a friend who chose to share. Up close, ask their screen to light up so you can spot them in the crowd.

Works where data doesn't

Phones talk directly to each other, and messages hop through other phones nearby to reach further. Friends out of range get them when they come back.

Nothing on a server

There is no account and no OffChat server. Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted and every message is signed, so nobody can pretend to be you.

How it works

Ready in a minute, before you lose the signal

  1. Add friends before you go

    While you're together and have internet, scan each other's code. The Get Ready checklist walks you through it in a minute.

  2. Lose the signal, keep the chat

    Messages travel over Bluetooth from phone to phone. Quick messages like “Where are you?” and “On my way” work in loud, dark places.

  3. Find each other

    Follow the arrow to a friend who shares with you. Save the meeting point, your tent or the car, and walk back to it the same way.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does OffChat need internet or a SIM card?

No. Phones talk directly over Bluetooth, and fast Wi-Fi connections between phones speed up photos and voice messages while the app is open. You only need internet once, to download the app.

How do messages reach people further away?

Every phone running OffChat can pass messages on, so a message can hop through other phones nearby to reach a friend you can't reach directly. Messages to friends out of range are sent when they come back.

Who can see where I am?

Only a friend you choose, and only the direction and distance, never a map. You can stop sharing at any time.

Can Android phones and iPhones chat with each other?

Yes. OffChat for Android is coming to Google Play, and it chats with OffChat on iPhone.

Is it free?

Yes. Chatting is free, with one free Find Friend search to try it. OffChat Plus is a one-time purchase with unlimited searches and more. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Can OffChat call for help?

“I need help” sends a message and your location to your friends. OffChat doesn't contact emergency services: in an emergency, call your local emergency number.
