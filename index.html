OffChat

Chat with people nearby. No internet needed.

OffChat sends messages directly between phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, so you can chat at festivals, on hikes, on flights, or anywhere the network is down. Messages can hop through other nearby phones to reach further.

  • No internet, no SIM card, no account
  • Channels and direct messages, with photos and voice messages
  • Encrypted direct messages and password-protected channels
  • No ads, no tracking, no servers
  • Available for Android and iPhone
