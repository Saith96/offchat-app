OffChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (changed: how long reports are kept)

In short

OffChat sends messages directly between phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. There is no OffChat server and no account. We (the developer) never receive your messages, contacts, photos, voice messages or profile. Everything you create in OffChat stays on your phone and on the phones you chat with.

Who we are

OffChat is made by an independent developer. Contact: saithdevelopments@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Your messages, photos and voice messages, your contacts and channels, and your profile (name, photo and bio).
  • Your identity keys. They are created on your phone and protected by the Android Keystore. They never leave your phone.

OffChat excludes this data from Android cloud backups. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also delete single messages, leave channels, or turn on disappearing messages.

What other people nearby can see

  • Your profile. While “Visible to people nearby” is on, phones in range running OffChat can see your name, profile photo and bio. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. Only you and the person you write to can read them.
  • Password-protected channels are encrypted with the channel password. Only people who know it can read them.
  • Open channels (without a password, such as “General”) are not encrypted. Anyone nearby who joins a channel with the same name can read them.
  • Relaying. To reach further, messages can hop through other OffChat users’ phones. Those phones can't read encrypted messages. A relaying phone may keep an encrypted message briefly in memory until the recipient comes into range.

Reporting a message or a person

You can report a message (long-press it) or a person (in their info screen). OffChat then opens an email draft to saithdevelopments@gmail.com in your email app. The draft contains the person's name and OffChat ID, the conversation, when the message was sent, the text of the message (up to 500 characters) and the app version. Photos and voice messages are never included. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself, and you can read and edit it first. We use reports only to look into the problem, and we keep them only as long as that takes, or longer where the law requires it (for example when we have to pass a report about illegal content to the authorities). If you also choose “Report and Block”, the person is blocked on your phone.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) and Wi-Fi: to find and connect to nearby phones. OffChat doesn't use these to determine your location.
  • Location (Android 12 and older only): Android requires it for Bluetooth scanning on those versions. OffChat doesn't read or store your location.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice message.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new messages.
  • Camera: OffChat uses Google's code scanner and your camera app to scan QR codes and take photos. It never gets direct access to your camera.

Google services

  • QR code scanning uses Google's ML Kit code scanner (part of Google Play services). Google may collect diagnostic and usage information about the scanner, such as device and app information and error reports. It does not receive the content of the codes you scan. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Nearby Connections (Google Play services) creates the Wi-Fi connections between phones.
  • OffChat Plus purchases are processed by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking by us, no selling or sharing of your data, and no collection of your messages or contacts.

Children

OffChat is not directed at children under 13. See our Child Safety Standards.

Changes and contact
